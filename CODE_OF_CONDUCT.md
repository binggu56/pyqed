# Code of Conduct

PyQED is committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration environment
for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome questions and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Give credit for code, data, methods, and review.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Disclose relevant conflicts of interest when reviewing scientific work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained
disruption, deliberate intimidation, and retaliation are not acceptable in
project spaces. Publishing private information or credentials is also
unacceptable.

## Scope and reporting

This policy applies in repository discussions, reviews, project events, and
other spaces where someone represents PyQED. Reports may be sent privately to
the project maintainer at gubing@westlake.edu.cn. Do not post sensitive details
in a public issue.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action. They should preserve reporter privacy as far as practical,
avoid conflicts of interest, and document public outcomes when doing so does
not expose private information.
